# Pilot Outreach Packet

This packet is for getting the first honest community or team trial. It should be used before any real-world impact claim.

## Who To Ask First

Good first operators:

- a subreddit or forum moderator
- an open-source maintainer
- a classroom or study community owner
- a multilingual support community lead
- a small team already using AI-assisted work

Avoid broad public claims. The goal is one real operator and one real workflow.

## Short Invite

```text
Hi, I am testing Coexistence Impact Engine, a small governance tool for communities where humans and AI participate together.

It does not try to detect or punish AI authorship. It helps an operator write AI participation rules, disclosure text, review criteria, multilingual explanations, and a simple evidence log.

Would you be willing to try it on one real policy or moderation workflow and tell me:

1. whether the packet was useful,
2. what you edited or rejected,
3. roughly how much time it saved or added,
4. whether the explanation would work for your users?

No private user data is needed.
```

## Baseline Questions

Ask before the operator uses the tool:

```text
1. What AI participation question is currently hard for your community or team?
2. Do you already have a written AI-use policy?
3. How long would it normally take to draft or revise that policy?
4. Where do decisions feel inconsistent today?
5. Which languages do you need user-facing explanations in?
6. What evidence would convince you that this workflow helped?
```

## After-Trial Evidence

Collect only non-private evidence:

```text
1. Was any policy text accepted?
2. What was edited or rejected?
3. How many decisions or explanations used the checklist?
4. Estimated minutes saved or added.
5. Any disputes avoided, clarified, or resolved.
6. Languages used.
7. One short operator quote, if they agree.
```

Use `docs/PILOT_EVIDENCE_TEMPLATE.md` after the trial so the evidence is recorded consistently and private details are removed before publication.

## Public Proof Shape

Safe public proof can be:

- a redacted operator quote
- accepted policy excerpt with private details removed
- count of decisions reviewed
- estimated minutes saved with method stated
- list of languages supported
- short note on what did not work

## Stoplines

- Do not include usernames, private messages, private moderation cases, or confidential community data.
- Do not claim broad impact from one trial.
- Do not claim AI-detection capability.
- Do not claim XPRIZE readiness until real usage evidence and business evidence exist.
